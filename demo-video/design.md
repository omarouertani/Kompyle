# Design — Kompyle demo video

Source: Kompyle's own dark theme (`src/app/globals.css`), sober "Linear-style" direction.
Voice: speaks to the viewer ("you"), a job seeker or someone who knows one.

## Acts

1. **Hook** (intro, 3.4 s): the problem, in two lines.
2. **Product** (six chapters, each a card then the app):
   1. a free AI key (4 of the 6 providers are free, model picker),
   2. paste an offer → the AI adapts the CV,
   3. cover letter,
   4. ATS score,
   5. edits on the page (toolbar: bold, highlight), templates, colours,
   6. export.
3. **Call to action** (outro, 6 s): free, open source, Docker, GitHub link, "send this to someone job hunting".

"Free & open source" is on screen the whole time: in every card footer and as a badge under the app.

## Colour

| Role | Value | App token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | `#262626` → `#1b1b1b` (diagonal) | `--background` / `--muted` (dark) |
| Ink | `#faf8f1` | `--card-foreground` (dark) |
| Soft ink | `#b7b5a6` | `--muted-foreground` (dark) |
| Accent | `#d87757` | `--primary` (dark) |
| Lines | `#3e3e38` | `--border` (dark) |

One accent: the terracotta goes on the second intro line, card kickers, click ripples, the badge dot and the GitHub URL.

## Type

- Display: Playfair Display Bold (the app's serif headings): 116 px intro, 112 px card titles (16:9), 150 px wordmark.
- Text: IBM Plex Sans (the app's body font), 40–52 px; subtitles 40 px (16:9), 38 px (1:1), 44 px (9:16).
- Card titles are two lines at most, broken by hand (`\n` in `lib/story.cjs`), on one left edge.
- Command: Consolas.

## Motion

- Card and intro words rise and sharpen one after the other (60–70 ms stagger).
- Transitions: [gl-transitions](https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions) (MIT), run as WebGL shaders in the
  compositor: `directionalwarp` into a card or the outro, `SimpleZoom` from a card into the app (0.75 s).
- A soft whoosh (filtered pink noise generated by ffmpeg) on each transition.

## Ground

The editor canvas's own grid (48 px, ink at 3.5 %), plus a faint terracotta glow top-left.

## Rules turned off

- **Gradient and drop shadow** on the app card: asked for in the brief (gradient background, rounded corners, shadow).
- **Effect transitions** (warp, zoom blur) instead of hard cuts: asked for ("modern, pro transitions").
